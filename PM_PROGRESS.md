# Product Management full courses: progress

Goal: every Product Management course as its own level, English and Bangla, same shape as the CSE courses (see CSE_PROGRESS.md).
The "Product Management" category (`pm`, assets/content/{en,bn}/pm.json) lists the course names only; no lessons written yet.

Status: `done` = full course in the app, `in progress` = lessons being written, `todo` = not started.
