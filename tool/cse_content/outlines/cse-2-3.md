# Course: Digital Logic Design  (level id "deep-cse-2-3", lesson ids "deep-cse-2-3-01".."-25")
01 Digital vs analog: signals, voltage levels as 0/1, why binary, digital systems around us, course map
02 Number systems: binary, octal, decimal, hexadecimal, conversions including fractions
03 Binary arithmetic and signed numbers: add/sub/multiply, sign-magnitude, 1's and 2's complement, overflow
04 Binary codes: BCD, excess-3, Gray code, ASCII/Unicode, parity bit, Hamming code idea
05 Logic gates: AND, OR, NOT, NAND, NOR, XOR, XNOR; symbols, truth tables, timing diagrams, universal gates
06 Boolean algebra: postulates, basic theorems, De Morgan, duality, consensus, algebraic simplification
07 Canonical and standard forms: minterms, maxterms, SOP/POS, conversion between forms, complement of a function
08 Karnaugh maps (2, 3, 4 variables): Gray order, grouping rules, wrap-around, worked simplifications
09 K-map deeper: don't-cares, prime and essential prime implicants, POS from K-map, 5-variable maps
10 Quine-McCluskey (tabular) method: grouping by ones, combining, prime implicant chart, Petrick idea
11 NAND/NOR implementation and multilevel logic: two-level forms, NAND-only, NOR-only, XOR/parity circuits
12 Combinational circuits: analysis and design procedure, code converters (BCD to excess-3), 7-segment
13 Adders and subtractors: half/full adder, ripple-carry adder, adder-subtractor, overflow detection, carry lookahead
14 BCD adder, binary multiplier, magnitude comparator
15 Decoders and encoders: 2-to-4, 3-to-8, enable, function implementation with decoders, priority encoder
16 Multiplexers and demultiplexers: 2:1, 4:1, 8:1, functions with MUX, tri-state buffers, buses
17 Hazards and timing in combinational circuits: propagation delay, static/dynamic hazards, glitches, fixing with K-map
18 Sequential logic and latches: memory idea, feedback, SR latch (NOR/NAND), gated SR, D latch
19 Flip-flops: edge triggering, master-slave, D, JK, T; characteristic and excitation tables; setup/hold time
20 Analysis of clocked sequential circuits: state equations, state table, state diagram, Mealy vs Moore
21 Design of sequential circuits: state reduction, state assignment, design with D/JK/T, sequence detector
22 Registers and shift registers: parallel load, SISO/SIPO/PISO/PIPO, universal shift register, serial transfer
23 Counters: ripple (asynchronous), synchronous up/down, mod-N and BCD counters, ring and Johnson counters
24 Memory and programmable logic: RAM/ROM organisation, ROM as logic, PLA, PAL, CPLD/FPGA intro; logic families (TTL, CMOS, fan-out, noise margin)
25 HDL and ASM charts: Verilog basics (module, assign, always), ASM charts, digital design flow, course recap
