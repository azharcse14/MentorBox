# Course 3: Discrete Mathematics  (level id "deep-cse-1-3", lesson ids "deep-cse-1-3-01".."-25")
01 What discrete maths is: discrete vs continuous, why CS needs it, map of the course, basic notation (sets of numbers, symbols)
02 Propositional logic: propositions, connectives (NOT, AND, OR, XOR, IF-THEN, IFF), truth tables, converse/inverse/contrapositive, translating English
03 Logical equivalences: tautology/contradiction/contingency, key laws (De Morgan, distributive, absorption...), simplifying, DNF/CNF, Boolean algebra link, satisfiability
04 Predicates and quantifiers: predicates, domain, for-all and there-exists, negating quantifiers, nested quantifiers, translating sentences
05 Rules of inference: valid arguments, modus ponens/tollens, syllogisms, resolution, fallacies, inference with quantifiers
06 Proof methods: direct, contrapositive, contradiction (sqrt 2 irrational), proof by cases, existence proofs, counterexamples, if-and-only-if proofs
07 Sets: notation, roster vs set-builder, empty set, subsets, power set, cardinality, Cartesian product, Venn diagrams
08 Set operations: union, intersection, difference, complement, symmetric difference, set identities, proving identities, bit-string sets, two-set inclusion-exclusion
09 Functions: domain/codomain/range, one-to-one, onto, bijection, inverse, composition, floor/ceiling, graphs of functions
10 Sequences and sums: arithmetic and geometric sequences, sigma notation, closed-form sums, countable vs uncountable sets (Cantor idea)
11 Mathematical induction: the domino idea, basis + inductive step, sum and divisibility examples, strong induction, well-ordering
12 Recursion: recursive definitions of sequences/sets/strings, structural induction, recursive algorithms (factorial, Fibonacci, binary search), growth (big-O mention)
13 Number theory I: divisibility, division algorithm, mod and congruence, modular arithmetic, primes, fundamental theorem of arithmetic, sieve of Eratosthenes
14 Number theory II: GCD and LCM, Euclidean algorithm, Bezout, modular inverse, linear congruences, Chinese remainder theorem
15 Number theory applications: hashing, check digits (ISBN), pseudorandom numbers, Caesar cipher, fast modular exponentiation, RSA idea
16 Counting basics: product, sum, subtraction and division rules, tree diagrams, pigeonhole principle (and generalized)
17 Permutations and combinations: P(n,r), C(n,r), binomial theorem, Pascal's triangle, repetition (stars and bars), identical objects
18 Discrete probability: sample space, events, equally likely outcomes, complement, conditional probability, independence, Bayes, expected value
19 Recurrence relations: modelling (Hanoi, Fibonacci), solving by iteration, linear homogeneous with constant coefficients, divide-and-conquer recurrences
20 Inclusion-exclusion: three sets, general principle, counting onto functions, derangements (hat-check problem)
21 Relations: binary relations, reflexive/symmetric/antisymmetric/transitive, matrix and digraph representation, composition, closures (Warshall idea), n-ary relations and databases
22 Equivalence relations and partial orders: equivalence classes, partitions, congruence mod n, posets, Hasse diagrams, total order, topological sort
23 Graphs basics: vertices/edges, simple/multi/directed, degree, handshaking theorem, Kn/Cn/bipartite, adjacency matrix and list, isomorphism
24 Graph paths and problems: connectivity, Euler paths/circuits (Konigsberg), Hamilton paths, shortest paths (Dijkstra), planar graphs (Euler formula), graph colouring
25 Trees: properties (n-1 edges), rooted and binary trees, BST, traversals, spanning trees (Prim, Kruskal), Huffman coding
