# Course: Linear Algebra  (level id "deep-cse-2-5", lesson ids "deep-cse-2-5-01".."-25")
01 What linear algebra is: scalars, vectors, matrices; why CS, graphics, AI and data science need it
02 Vectors: arrows and lists, R^2/R^3/R^n, addition, scalar multiplication, linear combinations
03 Dot product and length: norm, unit vectors, angle between vectors, orthogonality, Cauchy-Schwarz, triangle inequality, projection
04 Cross product, lines and planes in 3D: right-hand rule, area, normal vectors, plane equations
05 Matrices: notation, order, types (square, zero, identity, diagonal, triangular, symmetric), addition, scalar multiple, transpose
06 Matrix multiplication: row-times-column rule, size rule, AB != BA, properties, powers, matrix-vector as combination of columns
07 Systems of linear equations: Ax = b, augmented matrix, geometry of solutions, consistent/inconsistent, one/none/infinitely many
08 Gaussian elimination: elementary row operations, row echelon form, pivots, back substitution
09 Gauss-Jordan and RREF: pivot and free variables, parametric solutions, homogeneous systems
10 Matrix inverse: definition, 2x2 formula, finding inverses with Gauss-Jordan, properties, elementary matrices, invertible matrix theorem (intro)
11 LU decomposition and computation: A = LU, solving with forward/back substitution, partial pivoting, operation counts
12 Determinants I: 2x2, 3x3 (Sarrus), minors, cofactors, cofactor expansion, triangular matrices
13 Determinants II: row operation effects, det(AB), det(A^T), adjoint and inverse, Cramer's rule, area/volume meaning
14 Vector spaces and subspaces: axioms, examples (R^n, polynomials, matrices), subspace test
15 Span and linear independence: spanning sets, dependence, testing with row reduction
16 Basis and dimension: basis, standard basis, dimension, coordinates relative to a basis
17 The four fundamental subspaces: column space, null space, row space, left null space, rank, rank-nullity theorem
18 Linear transformations: definition, matrix of a transformation, kernel and range, one-to-one and onto
19 Geometric transformations for graphics: scaling, rotation, reflection, shear, composition, homogeneous coordinates, translation
20 Change of basis and similar matrices: transition matrices, B^-1 A B, why a good basis helps
21 Orthogonality: orthogonal and orthonormal sets, orthogonal matrices, Gram-Schmidt, QR factorization
22 Least squares: inconsistent systems, projection onto a subspace, normal equations A^T A x = A^T b, line fitting
23 Eigenvalues and eigenvectors: Av = lambda v, characteristic equation, computing for 2x2 and 3x3, trace and determinant checks
24 Diagonalization and applications: A = PDP^-1, matrix powers, Markov chains, PageRank idea, symmetric matrices and spectral theorem
25 SVD, PCA and linear algebra in computing: singular values, low-rank image compression, PCA, recommenders, course recap
