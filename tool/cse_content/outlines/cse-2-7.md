# Course: Complex Variables, Fourier & Laplace Transforms (Math III)  (level id "deep-cse-2-7", lesson ids "deep-cse-2-7-01".."-25")
01 Complex numbers from scratch: why i exists, a + ib, real/imaginary parts, arithmetic, conjugate, Argand plane
02 Polar form: modulus, argument (principal value), Euler's formula e^(iθ), multiplication as rotate-and-scale, De Moivre's theorem
03 Roots and regions: nth roots of a complex number, roots of unity, circles, disks, annuli, half-planes, domains
04 Elementary complex functions: e^z, sin z, cos z, sinh/cosh, Log z (principal value, branches), complex powers
05 Limits, continuity and the complex derivative: why direction matters, differentiation rules, where z-bar fails
06 Analytic functions and the Cauchy-Riemann equations (Cartesian and polar), entire functions, tests and examples
07 Harmonic functions: Laplace's equation, harmonic conjugates, building f(z) from u (Milne-Thomson idea)
08 Conformal mapping: angle preservation, w = z², 1/z, e^z, bilinear (Mobius) transformations, fixed points, cross-ratio
09 Complex line integrals: curves and contours, parametrisation, worked integrals, ML-inequality, path dependence
10 Cauchy's integral theorem and Cauchy's integral formula (incl. derivatives), Liouville and fundamental theorem of algebra
11 Power series, Taylor series and Laurent series: radius of convergence, expansions in annuli, worked examples
12 Zeros and singularities: removable, poles (order), essential; computing residues (simple poles, order m, Laurent)
13 The residue theorem: statement, worked contour integrals, multiple poles inside a curve
14 Real integrals by residues: integrals of trig functions over 0..2π, rational functions over the real line, Jordan's lemma idea
15 Periodic functions and Fourier series: orthogonality of sin/cos, Euler formulas for a0, an, bn
16 Fourier series worked examples: square wave, sawtooth, |x|; Dirichlet conditions, jumps, Gibbs phenomenon
17 Even/odd functions, half-range sine and cosine series, functions of any period 2L
18 Complex form of Fourier series, Parseval's identity, line spectrum, energy of a signal
19 From series to transform: Fourier integral, Fourier transform and inverse, Fourier sine/cosine transforms
20 Fourier transform properties: linearity, shifting, scaling, modulation, convolution theorem, standard pairs, DFT/FFT pointer
21 The Laplace transform: definition, existence (piecewise continuous, exponential order), table of basic transforms
22 Laplace properties: linearity, first shifting, multiplication/division by t, derivatives and integrals, unit step, second shifting, impulse, periodic functions
23 Inverse Laplace transform: tables, partial fractions, completing the square, convolution theorem, initial/final value theorems
24 Solving ODEs with Laplace: initial value problems, systems of ODEs, RLC circuits, spring-mass examples
25 Putting it together for CSE: signals, systems, transfer functions, poles and stability, filters, Z-transform pointer, wrap-up
