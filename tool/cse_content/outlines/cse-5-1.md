# Course: Artificial Intelligence  (level id "deep-cse-5-1", lesson ids "deep-cse-5-1-01".."-25")
Builds on Discrete Mathematics (deep-cse-1-3: propositional logic, quantifiers, rules of inference), Data Structures (deep-cse-2-2: queues,
stacks, priority queues, plain BFS/DFS), Algorithms (deep-cse-3-1: Dijkstra, complexity) and Probability & Statistics (deep-cse-2-6:
conditional probability, Bayes' theorem). Those are only recapped; here the focus is how an AI agent uses them. Machine Learning (deep-cse-6-1),
Deep Learning, NLP and Computer Vision have their own later courses, so lessons 24-25 stay at overview level. Follows the classic
Russell & Norvig style syllabus: agents, search, games, CSPs, logic, planning, uncertainty.
01 What AI is: four definitions (think/act humanly/rationally), Turing test, narrow vs general AI, short history (Dartmouth 1956, AI winters, expert systems, machine learning, deep learning), AI around us in Bangladesh, course map
02 Intelligent agents: agent, sensors, actuators, percepts, percept sequence, agent function vs agent program, rationality and performance measure, rational vs omniscient, autonomy, PEAS with worked examples (taxi, vacuum world)
03 Environments and agent architectures: observable, deterministic/stochastic, episodic/sequential, static/dynamic, discrete/continuous, single/multi-agent, known/unknown; simple reflex, model-based reflex, goal-based, utility-based and learning agents
04 Problem formulation: problem-solving agents, initial state, actions, transition model, goal test, path cost, state space, abstraction; examples (route finding on a Bangladesh map, 8-puzzle, water jug, 8-queens), counting states
05 The search framework: search tree vs state space, nodes (state, parent, action, path cost, depth), frontier, tree search vs graph search with explored set, repeated states, measuring strategies (completeness, optimality, time, space; b, d, m)
06 Breadth-first and uniform-cost search: FIFO frontier, BFS trace, O(b^d) time and space with a numbers table, when BFS is optimal, UCS with priority queue on g(n), UCS trace on a weighted map, goal test on expansion, link to Dijkstra
07 Depth-first, depth-limited, iterative deepening and bidirectional search: LIFO frontier, DFS trace, O(bm) memory, incompleteness, depth limit, IDS trace and why repeated work is cheap, bidirectional idea, comparison of all uninformed strategies
08 Heuristics and greedy best-first search: heuristic function h(n), straight-line distance, greedy best-first trace and why it is not optimal, designing heuristics for the 8-puzzle (misplaced tiles, Manhattan distance), relaxed problems, dominance
09 A* search: f(n) = g(n) + h(n), full worked trace, admissible and consistent heuristics, why admissible A* is optimal (idea), effective branching factor, memory problem, IDA* and weighted A* briefly
10 Local search and hill climbing: when the path does not matter, state landscape, objective function, steepest-ascent hill climbing on 8-queens, local maxima, plateaus, ridges, sideways moves, random-restart and stochastic hill climbing
11 Simulated annealing, local beam search and genetic algorithms: temperature, accepting worse moves with probability e^(dE/T), cooling schedule, local beam search, GA with population, fitness, selection, crossover, mutation, worked example
12 Adversarial search and minimax: games as search, two-player zero-sum games, MAX and MIN, game tree, utility, minimax worked example, tic-tac-toe, time O(b^m), why full search is impossible for chess
13 Alpha-beta pruning and real game programs: alpha and beta meaning, step-by-step pruning trace, move ordering and best case O(b^(m/2)), depth cutoff, evaluation functions, horizon effect, chance games (expectiminimax), Monte Carlo tree search idea
14 Constraint satisfaction problems: variables, domains, constraints, assignment, consistent and complete solutions, map colouring of Bangladesh divisions, Sudoku, exam timetabling, constraint graph, unary/binary/global constraints
15 Solving CSPs: backtracking search, MRV, degree heuristic, least constraining value, forward checking trace, arc consistency and AC-3 worked example, min-conflicts local search
16 Knowledge-based agents and propositional logic: knowledge base, TELL/ASK, wumpus world, syntax and semantics recap, models, entailment, model checking with truth tables, validity and satisfiability
17 Inference in propositional logic: inference rules, soundness and completeness, CNF conversion, resolution refutation worked example, Horn clauses, forward chaining and backward chaining traces
18 First-order logic: objects, relations, functions, constants, variables, quantifiers, translating English into FOL (family, university examples), common mistakes with quantifiers, why FOL is more expressive than propositional logic
19 Inference in first-order logic: substitution, universal and existential instantiation, unification worked examples, generalized modus ponens, forward and backward chaining in FOL, resolution in FOL briefly, Prolog basics
20 Knowledge representation and expert systems: ontology, categories and inheritance, semantic networks, frames, production rules, expert systems (knowledge base, inference engine, explanation), MYCIN, strengths and limits
21 Classical planning: planning vs search, STRIPS/PDDL representation (preconditions, add and delete lists), blocks world, forward and backward planning, planning heuristics idea, partial-order planning and real uses
22 Uncertainty and probability: why logic fails under uncertainty, degrees of belief, random variables, joint distribution, marginal and conditional probability, independence, Bayes' rule worked examples, naive Bayes spam filter
23 Bayesian networks: nodes and directed edges, conditional probability tables, chain rule factorisation, burglar alarm example worked, conditional independence, exact inference by enumeration, sampling idea, uses
24 Introduction to machine learning and neural networks: learning from examples, supervised/unsupervised/reinforcement, decision tree idea, training and test data, overfitting, perceptron worked example, multilayer networks and backpropagation idea, deep learning (overview only)
25 NLP, computer vision, AI ethics and wrap-up: NLP tasks and Bangla language challenges, language models at a high level, vision tasks (classification, detection), bias, privacy, explainability, jobs, safety, responsible use, course recap and what next
