# Course: Multimedia Systems  (level id "deep-cse-5-9", lesson ids "deep-cse-5-9-01".."-26")
Builds on Digital Signal Processing (deep-cse-5-7: sampling, aliasing, quantization, SQNR, PCM bit rate, DFT/FFT, the 8x8 DCT named),
Computer Graphics (deep-cse-5-2: RGB/CMY/HSV colour models, raster vs vector, frame buffer, textures, animation basics),
Data Communication (deep-cse-3-6: PCM, bandwidth, error control) and Computer Networks (deep-cse-4-3: TCP/UDP, HTTP, CDNs, DASH first look).
Those are only recapped. Here the focus is the multimedia side: how text, images, audio and video are represented, how they are
compressed (lossless and lossy, JPEG, MP3/AAC, motion-compensated video), how they are packaged and streamed, and how they are stored,
searched, authored and protected. Game Development (cse-5-10) covers game engines and real-time interaction; only mentioned here.
Every worked number is checked with a script. Codec and standard claims are kept version-neutral ("typically", "roughly").
01 What multimedia is: media types (text, image, graphics, audio, video, animation), static vs continuous (time-based) media, linear vs interactive and hypermedia, components of a multimedia system (capture, storage, processing, transmission, presentation), why raw media is huge (1080p30 raw about 746 Mbps), the file size formula, KB vs KiB, course map
02 Text, fonts and Unicode: characters vs glyphs, ASCII (7-bit) and code pages, Unicode code points, UTF-8/UTF-16/UTF-32 worked (A = 41, অ U+0985 = E0 A6 85, ক্ষ = 3 code points = 9 bytes), bitmap vs outline fonts (TrueType/OpenType), kerning and leading, Bangla script shaping (vowel sign ি drawn before the consonant, hasanta and conjuncts), legacy Bijoy-style ANSI fonts vs Unicode
03 Digital images: pixels and sampling of a scene, resolution, bit depth (1, 8, 24, 32 with alpha), binary/grayscale/indexed/true colour, palette (colour lookup table), file size worked (1920x1080x24 = 6,220,800 bytes, about 5.93 MiB; 640x480 1-bit = 38,400 bytes), PPI/DPI (A4 at 300 dpi = 2480x3508, about 26.1 MB raw), raster vs vector recap, image histogram
04 Colour in images and video: RGB recap, why separate brightness, Y'CbCr (BT.601 Y = 0.299R + 0.587G + 0.114B; worked (200,100,50) -> Y about 124, Cb about 86, Cr about 182), eye more sensitive to luma detail, chroma subsampling 4:4:4/4:2:2/4:2:0 worked (24 -> 16 -> 12 bits per pixel, 1080p frame 6.22 MB -> 3.11 MB), gamma in one paragraph, BT.709 named
05 Digital audio: sound as pressure waves, frequency/pitch, amplitude/loudness, decibels, hearing range about 20 Hz-20 kHz, PCM recap (from DSP), common sample rates (8, 16, 44.1, 48 kHz), bit depth, channels (mono, stereo, 5.1), bit rate worked (CD 1,411,200 bps; 3-minute song 31,752,000 bytes; phone 64 kbps), WAV, MIDI vs sampled audio, speech vs music
06 Information theory basics: redundancy types (spatial, temporal, statistical/coding, perceptual), self-information -log2 p, entropy worked (0.5/0.25/0.125/0.125 -> 1.75 bits; 0.4/0.3/0.2/0.1 -> about 1.846; 0.9/0.1 -> about 0.469), source coding bound, compression ratio and bits per pixel, lossless vs lossy definitions
07 Run-length and Huffman coding: RLE worked (12W 1B 12W 3B), when RLE expands, prefix-free codes, Huffman tree built for AAAABBBCCD (A 0, B 10, C 110, D 111 -> 19 bits vs 20 fixed, average 1.9 vs entropy 1.846), decoding, why Huffman cannot beat 1 bit per symbol, canonical/adaptive Huffman named
08 Dictionary coding: LZ77 sliding window and (offset, length, next) idea, LZ78/LZW worked (ABABABA with A=1, B=2 -> outputs 1 2 3 5, dictionary AB=3, BA=4, ABA=5), decoding and the special case of a code not yet in the table, GIF uses LZW, DEFLATE = LZ77 + Huffman (ZIP, gzip, PNG)
09 Arithmetic coding and predictive coding: one number for the whole message, interval narrowing worked (P(A)=0.8, P(B)=0.2, AAB -> [0.512, 0.64), about 2.97 bits of information), why it beats Huffman for skewed sources, CABAC named, differential/predictive coding (DPCM idea, PNG filters, lossless JPEG idea), delta encoding of sensor data
10 Lossy compression principles: why throw away data, rate-distortion trade-off, quantization as the only lossy step, MSE and PSNR worked (8-bit, MSE 10 -> 38.13 dB, MSE 100 -> 28.13 dB), transform coding and energy compaction, perceptual coding, subjective quality (MOS), generation loss
11 The discrete cosine transform: why a transform helps, 1D DCT of 8 samples, basis patterns, 2D 8x8 DCT, DC and AC coefficients, worked flat block (all 100, level shift -> DC = -224) and horizontal gradient row (96..152 step 8), why DCT not DFT (no wrap-around jump), separability, link to DSP
12 The JPEG pipeline: colour conversion and 4:2:0, 8x8 blocks, level shift, DCT, quantization table (luma DC step 16), worked gradient block (DC -32 -> -2, first AC -145.77/11 -> -13, (0,3) -15.24/16 -> -1), zigzag, DC differential, AC (run, value) pairs (0,-13) (4,-1) EOB, Huffman, decoding (max error about 1 here), quality factor, blocking and ringing, progressive JPEG, JPEG 2000 named
13 Image formats compared: BMP (raw), GIF (256-colour palette, LZW, 1-bit transparency, animation), PNG (lossless, DEFLATE, filters, alpha), JPEG (photos, lossy, no alpha), WebP (lossy and lossless, alpha, animation), AVIF/HEIC named, SVG vector; choosing per use (photo, screenshot, logo, sticker), metadata (EXIF) and privacy
14 Psychoacoustics and audio compression: absolute threshold of hearing, frequency masking, temporal masking, critical bands, perceptual coder block diagram (filter bank, psychoacoustic model, bit allocation, bitstream), MP3 (MPEG-1/2 Audio Layer III), AAC, typical 128 kbps vs 1411.2 kbps (about 11:1), Opus and speech codecs, lossless FLAC
15 Video basics: video as a sequence of frames, frame rate (24, 25, 30, 50, 60), progressive vs interlaced (fields, combing), resolutions (SD, 720p, 1080p, 4K UHD 3840x2160), aspect ratio, raw bit rate worked (1080p30 4:2:0 8-bit = 746,496,000 bps, one hour about 336 GB), PAL/NTSC history (Bangladesh broadcast was PAL), why compression is mandatory
16 Video compression and frame types: spatial vs temporal redundancy, intra coding, I/P/B frames, GOP structure (IBBPBBP...), display order vs decode order, random access and seeking, error propagation, residual coding, macroblocks
17 Motion estimation and block matching: motion compensation idea, motion vectors, block matching with SAD/MSE, worked tiny SAD search, search window +-p gives (2p+1)^2 positions (p = 7: 225 positions x 256 = 57,600 differences per 16x16 block), three-step search (25 positions), sub-pixel motion, motion vector prediction
18 Video codec standards: who makes standards (ITU-T, ISO/IEC MPEG, AOMedia), MPEG-1, MPEG-2, MPEG-4 Part 2, H.264/AVC, H.265/HEVC, VP9, AV1, H.266/VVC named; what improves each generation (bigger and flexible blocks, more intra modes, better entropy coding, in-loop filters); compression vs encoder complexity; licensing and hardware decoding (stated qualitatively)
19 Containers vs codecs: codec = how media is compressed, container = how streams are packaged; MP4, MKV, WebM, MOV, AVI, MPEG-TS; tracks, muxing/demuxing, timestamps (PTS/DTS), lip sync, subtitles and metadata, "file plays but no sound" explained, ffmpeg/ffprobe in concept
20 Multimedia networking and streaming: stored vs live vs interactive, download vs progressive download vs streaming, playout buffer worked (2 Mbps video, 1.5 Mbps link, 10 minutes -> wait 200 s), jitter and the jitter buffer, packet loss and concealment, FEC idea, TCP vs UDP for media, RTP (sequence numbers, timestamps), RTCP, RTSP as a remote control
21 Adaptive bitrate streaming: HTTP-based streaming, segments, HLS (m3u8 playlists) and MPEG-DASH (MPD), bitrate ladder, throughput-based vs buffer-based switching, CDNs, data use worked (1 Mbps for 1 hour = 450 MB), startup delay vs rebuffering, low-latency variants named
22 QoS and video conferencing: QoS parameters (bandwidth, delay, jitter, loss), one-way delay guidance (ITU-T G.114 about 150 ms), best effort vs IntServ vs DiffServ, scheduling recap, conferencing architectures (mesh: 5 people -> 20 streams, SFU, MCU), WebRTC, SIP named, echo cancellation, simulcast and SVC
23 Multimedia databases and retrieval: why ordinary tables are not enough, storing BLOBs vs file paths, metadata/text-based vs content-based retrieval, features (colour histogram, texture, shape), histogram intersection worked (0.7), similarity and k-nearest neighbours, indexing high-dimensional data, audio fingerprinting idea, video shots and keyframes, precision and recall
24 Authoring tools and AR/VR media: authoring paradigms (timeline, card/page, icon/flow), storyboards and scripts, HTML5 (video, audio, canvas) as an authoring target, accessibility (captions, alt text), 360-degree video and equirectangular projection, stereoscopic VR, high frame rate and low latency for comfort, 3D model formats (glTF named), AR overlays
25 Copyright, DRM and ethics: copyright basics and exceptions (fair use/fair dealing idea), Creative Commons licences, DRM (encryption, licence servers, Widevine/FairPlay/PlayReady named), watermarking and fingerprinting, deepfakes and manipulated media, privacy (EXIF location, face data), accessibility and inclusion
26 Capstone and wrap-up: planning a low-bandwidth video-lecture platform for Bangladesh: content types, encoding ladder, slide+audio mode, data per hour per rung, HLS/DASH delivery via CDN, offline downloads, captions in Bangla and English, Unicode search, cost estimate, course recap and what next
