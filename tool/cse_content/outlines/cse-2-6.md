# Course: Probability & Statistics  (level id "deep-cse-2-6", lesson ids "deep-cse-2-6-01".."-25")
01 What probability and statistics are: data, population vs sample, variables (qualitative/quantitative, discrete/continuous), scales of measurement, descriptive vs inferential
02 Collecting and showing data: raw data, frequency tables, class intervals, relative/cumulative frequency, bar/pie charts, histogram, frequency polygon, ogive, stem-and-leaf
03 Measures of central tendency: arithmetic mean (raw, grouped, weighted), geometric and harmonic mean, median, mode, grouped-data formulas, which one to use
04 Measures of dispersion: range, quartiles, percentiles, IQR, mean deviation, variance and standard deviation (population vs sample, n-1), coefficient of variation, box plot, outliers
05 Moments, skewness and kurtosis: raw and central moments, Pearson's coefficients of skewness, positive/negative skew, kurtosis (lepto/meso/platy)
06 Counting techniques: multiplication rule, factorial, permutations (with repetition, identical items), combinations, Pascal's triangle, worked problems
07 Basic probability: experiment, sample space, events, set operations, classical/relative-frequency/axiomatic definitions, Kolmogorov axioms
08 Probability rules: complement, addition rule, mutually exclusive events, independent events, multiplication rule, the birthday problem
09 Conditional probability and Bayes' theorem: P(A|B), tree diagrams, law of total probability, Bayes' theorem, medical test and spam filter examples
10 Random variables: discrete vs continuous, PMF, PDF, CDF and their properties, worked examples with dice and spinners
11 Expectation and variance of random variables: E[X], E[g(X)], linearity, Var(X) = E[X^2]-(E[X])^2, properties, moment generating function idea, Chebyshev's inequality
12 Bernoulli and binomial distributions: Bernoulli trial, binomial PMF, mean np, variance np(1-p), worked problems (MCQ guessing, defective items)
13 Poisson, geometric and hypergeometric distributions: Poisson PMF and mean = variance, Poisson approximation to binomial, geometric (first success), hypergeometric (no replacement)
14 Continuous uniform and exponential distributions: uniform PDF/CDF/mean/variance, exponential PDF/CDF, mean 1/lambda, memoryless property, link with Poisson
15 The normal distribution: bell curve, parameters, standard normal Z, z-table, 68-95-99.7 rule, normal approximation to binomial with continuity correction
16 Joint distributions: joint PMF/PDF tables, marginal and conditional distributions, independence, covariance, correlation coefficient of random variables, Var(X+Y)
17 Law of large numbers and the central limit theorem: sample mean, weak LLN idea, CLT statement, standard error sigma/sqrt(n), worked examples
18 Sampling and sampling distributions: random sampling methods (simple, stratified, systematic, cluster), bias, sampling distribution of mean and proportion, t, chi-square and F distributions
19 Point estimation: estimator vs estimate, unbiasedness, efficiency, consistency, method of moments, maximum likelihood estimation with simple examples
20 Confidence intervals: meaning, z interval for mean, t interval, interval for a proportion, margin of error, choosing sample size
21 Hypothesis testing basics: null and alternative hypotheses, test statistic, significance level, p-value, critical region, one- vs two-tailed, Type I and Type II errors, power
22 Tests for means and proportions: one-sample z and t tests, two-sample t test, paired t test, test for a proportion, step-by-step worked examples
23 Chi-square tests and ANOVA: goodness of fit, contingency tables and test of independence, expected counts, one-way ANOVA idea and F ratio
24 Correlation and regression: scatter plots, Pearson's r, Spearman's rank correlation, least squares line, slope and intercept formulas, R squared, prediction, correlation vs causation
25 Probability in computer science: Markov chains (transition matrix, steady state), queues (M/M/1 basics), randomized algorithms, hashing, A/B testing, Naive Bayes; course recap
