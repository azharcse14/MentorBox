# Course: Operations Research  (level id "deep-cse-6-10", lesson ids "deep-cse-6-10-01".."-25")
Builds on Linear Algebra (deep-cse-2-5: systems of equations, Gaussian elimination, matrices and inverses), Probability & Statistics
(deep-cse-2-6: Poisson and exponential distributions, normal distribution, expected value), Algorithms (deep-cse-3-1: greedy, dynamic
programming, Dijkstra, MST, max flow, NP-hardness) and Graph Theory (deep-cse-3-9). Those are only recapped; here the focus is turning
messy real decisions into models and solving them to optimality: linear programming and the simplex method, duality and sensitivity,
transportation and assignment, integer programming, network and project models, dynamic programming, inventory, queues, games and decisions.
Later courses go deeper, so keep these light and point forward: Simulation & Modeling (deep-cse-6-5, for systems too random for formulas),
Software Project Management (deep-cse-6-11, scheduling in practice), Advanced Algorithms (deep-cse-7-1, LP in approximation algorithms),
Machine Learning (deep-cse-6-1, gradient descent) and Reinforcement Learning (deep-cse-8-4, Markov decision processes). Taha / Hillier and
Lieberman style, every worked number (corner points, tableaus, transport costs, Hungarian assignments, CPM floats, EOQ, queue formulas,
game values) checked with Python. Solver names (Excel Solver, PuLP, OR-Tools) appear only as version-neutral examples.
01 What operations research is: decisions under limits, origins (WWII radar and convoys, Dantzig and simplex 1947), the OR approach (define problem, build model, solve, validate, implement), deterministic vs stochastic models, optimisation vs simulation vs heuristics, model = simplification, where OR is used (airlines, garments, logistics, bKash-style agent cash, hospitals), course map
02 Formulating linear programs: decision variables, objective, constraints, non-negativity, the four LP assumptions (proportionality, additivity, divisibility, certainty), worked formulations (Gazipur garments product mix, cattle-feed diet/blending, bus-driver shift scheduling, rice-mill capital budgeting), units check, common formulation mistakes
03 Graphical method: plotting constraints as lines and half-planes, feasible region, corner (extreme) points, iso-profit line method, corner point enumeration worked (max and min problems), binding vs non-binding constraints, special cases (infeasible, unbounded, alternative optima, redundant constraints), why the optimum is at a corner
04 Standard form and basic solutions: converting any LP (min to max, >= with surplus, = constraints, free and negative variables), slack variables meaning leftover resource, basic and non-basic variables, basic solutions and basic feasible solutions, C(n,m) counting worked, corner points = BFS, why searching all corners is hopeless at scale
05 The simplex method: the idea (walk along edges uphill), initial tableau, entering variable (most negative in Z-row), minimum ratio test and leaving variable, pivoting with row operations, full worked example 2 iterations, reading the final tableau, optimality condition, simplex for minimisation
06 Simplex special cases and artificial variables: >= and = constraints need artificial variables, Big-M method worked, two-phase method idea, detecting infeasibility, unboundedness (no positive ratio), alternative optima (zero reduced cost for a non-basic variable), degeneracy (tie in ratio test, zero RHS), cycling and Bland's rule, revised simplex and B-inverse brief, complexity (Klee-Minty worst case, ellipsoid 1979, interior point Karmarkar 1984)
07 Duality: every LP has a twin, building the dual (primal-dual conversion table), worked dual of the garments LP, weak duality, strong duality, complementary slackness worked, economic meaning (shadow price = value of one more unit of resource), reading duals from the final tableau
08 Sensitivity analysis and dual simplex: why the data is never exact, ranging objective coefficients worked, ranging right-hand sides and shadow price validity, reduced costs, adding a new product (pricing out), adding a constraint, dual simplex method worked, 100% rule brief, what solver sensitivity reports show
09 The transportation problem: formulation (supply, demand, cost matrix), balanced vs unbalanced (dummy row/column), m+n-1 basic cells, initial solutions worked on one 3x4 problem: northwest corner, least cost, Vogel's approximation method, comparing their costs
10 Optimising transportation: MODI (u-v) method worked, opportunity costs, closed loop and theta adjustment, stepping-stone idea, degeneracy and epsilon, maximisation transport, prohibited routes (big M), transshipment brief
11 The assignment problem: n jobs to n workers, why it is a special transportation problem, Hungarian method step by step (row reduction, column reduction, cover zeros with minimum lines, adjust), full worked 4x4, unbalanced (dummy), maximisation (subtract from max), forbidden assignments, travelling salesman contrast
12 Integer programming: why rounding fails (worked), pure vs mixed vs 0-1, modelling with binary variables (knapsack, fixed charge, either-or, if-then, at most k of n), branch and bound worked on a small IP with tree, bounding and pruning, cutting planes (Gomory) idea, NP-hard warning and solver gaps
13 Network models I: graphs, nodes and arcs recap, shortest path (Dijkstra recap with worked Dhaka road network, shortest path as LP), minimum spanning tree (Kruskal/Prim recap, rural fibre/electric line example), when to use which
14 Network models II, maximum flow and min-cost flow: flow networks, capacities and conservation, augmenting paths and residual graph, Ford-Fulkerson worked, max-flow min-cut theorem worked, min-cost flow idea and how transportation/assignment/shortest path are special cases, totally unimodular brief (why integer answers come free)
15 Project scheduling with CPM: activities, precedence, activity-on-node network, forward pass (ES, EF), backward pass (LS, LF), total float and free float, critical path worked on a 7-activity app-launch project, Gantt chart, what delays do
16 PERT and project crashing: uncertain durations, optimistic/most likely/pessimistic, mean (a + 4m + b)/6 and variance ((b - a)/6)^2 worked, project mean and variance on critical path, probability of finishing by a deadline with z-score worked, limitations, crashing: normal vs crash time and cost, cost slope, step-by-step crashing worked, time-cost trade-off
17 Dynamic programming in OR: stages, states, decisions, Bellman's principle of optimality, backward recursion, stagecoach (Dhaka to Cox's Bazar) worked, resource allocation worked (distributing salespeople/doctors), 0-1 knapsack as DP, curse of dimensionality, link to MDPs (deep-cse-8-4)
18 Inventory models I, EOQ: why hold stock, ordering cost vs holding cost, EOQ derivation idea and formula worked (pharmacy example), total cost curve, number of orders and cycle time, reorder point with lead time, sensitivity (flat cost curve), assumptions
19 Inventory models II: production order quantity (EPQ) worked, planned shortages brief, quantity discounts worked, safety stock and service level with normal demand worked, ABC analysis, periodic vs continuous review, newsvendor model worked (daily newspapers/fresh fish)
20 Queuing theory I: why queues form, components (arrival process, service, servers, discipline, capacity, population), Kendall notation, Poisson arrivals and exponential service recap, utilisation rho, Little's law worked, M/M/1 formulas (L, Lq, W, Wq, P0, Pn) worked on a bKash agent shop, why waiting explodes as rho -> 1
21 Queuing theory II: M/M/c with Erlang C worked (bank counters), M/M/1/K finite queue worked, pooling (one line vs separate lines), cost model (server cost vs waiting cost) worked, M/G/1 Pollaczek-Khinchine brief, when to switch to simulation (deep-cse-6-5)
22 Game theory: two-person zero-sum games, payoff matrix, maximin and minimax, saddle point worked, dominance, mixed strategies, 2x2 formula worked, graphical method for 2xn, solving a game by LP, beyond zero-sum (prisoner's dilemma, Nash equilibrium brief)
23 Decision analysis: decisions under certainty, risk and uncertainty, criteria (maximax, maximin, Laplace, Hurwicz, minimax regret) worked on one payoff table, expected monetary value, expected value of perfect information, decision trees with a test/survey branch worked, utility and risk attitude brief
24 Nonlinear optimisation and metaheuristics: when linear is not enough, convex vs non-convex, local vs global optimum, unconstrained optimum by derivatives, Lagrange multipliers worked, KKT intuition, gradient descent link (deep-cse-6-1), heuristics for hard problems (nearest neighbour TSP, 2-opt), local search, simulated annealing, genetic algorithms, tabu brief, vehicle routing in Dhaka
25 OR in practice and capstone: the modelling workflow, data and validation, solvers (Excel Solver, PuLP, OR-Tools as examples) and a tiny LP written in code, reading solver output, capstone: a Bangladesh FMCG distributor (invented "DeshMart") deciding production mix, depot-to-shop transport, delivery routes, stock levels and counter staffing, how each lesson's tool fits, recap and what next (deep-cse-6-5, deep-cse-6-11, deep-cse-7-1, deep-cse-8-4)
