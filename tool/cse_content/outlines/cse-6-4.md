# Course: Digital Image Processing  (level id "deep-cse-6-4", lesson ids "deep-cse-6-4-01".."-26")
Builds on Linear Algebra (deep-cse-2-5: lessons 06 matrix multiplication, 18-19 linear and geometric transformations, 25 SVD/PCA),
Probability & Statistics (deep-cse-2-6: distributions, mean and variance), Math III (deep-cse-2-7: lessons 19-20 Fourier transform and
convolution theorem), Signals & Systems (deep-cse-3-7: lessons 08-11 convolution, 19 sampling and aliasing), Computer Graphics
(deep-cse-5-2: lessons 01 pixels, 09 homogeneous matrices, 19 colour models, 23 texture filtering), Digital Signal Processing
(deep-cse-5-7: lessons 15-17 DFT, FFT, circular convolution; 19-20 filter design) and Multimedia Systems (deep-cse-5-9: lessons 03-04
pixels and YCbCr, 06-12 entropy, Huffman, DCT and the JPEG pipeline). Those are only recapped; here everything is done in 2D on real
pixel grids. Later courses go further, so keep these light and point forward: Pattern Recognition (deep-cse-6-12), Computer Vision
(deep-cse-8-3), Deep Learning (deep-cse-8-1), Machine Learning recap (deep-cse-6-1). Gonzalez & Woods style syllabus, with small
pixel matrices worked by hand and every number checked with Python. OpenCV/NumPy appear only as version-neutral examples.
01 What digital image processing is: an image as a function f(x, y) and a grid of numbers, low/mid/high-level processing, DIP vs computer graphics vs computer vision, short history (Bartlane cable pictures 1920s, space probe images 1960s, CT scans 1970s), images across the electromagnetic spectrum (gamma, X-ray, UV, visible, infrared, microwave/radar, radio/MRI), the fundamental steps, components of an image processing system, Bangladesh uses, course map
02 Human vision and image formation: structure of the eye (cornea, lens, retina, cones about 6-7 million, rods about 75-150 million, fovea, blind spot), brightness adaptation, Weber ratio worked, Mach bands and simultaneous contrast, light and the EM spectrum (wavelength, frequency, photon energy), radiance/luminance/brightness, sensors (single, strip, array, CCD vs CMOS, Bayer filter), the image formation model f = i x r with typical numbers
03 Sampling and quantization: continuous scene to digital image, sampling (spatial grid) and quantization (gray levels), L = 2^k levels, storage b = M x N x k worked (1024 x 1024 x 8 = 1 MiB, colour x3), spatial resolution (dpi, line pairs), intensity resolution and false contouring (256 to 8 levels), aliasing and moire patterns in 2D (recap deep-cse-3-7 lesson 19), dynamic range and contrast, saturation and noise
04 Pixel relationships and basic operations: coordinate convention (origin top-left, row x column), neighbours N4, ND, N8, adjacency (4, 8, m-adjacency and why m fixes ambiguous paths), paths, connectivity, regions, boundaries, distance measures (Euclidean, city-block D4, chessboard D8) worked, element-wise vs matrix operations, linear vs nonlinear operators, arithmetic (averaging K noisy images reduces noise variance by K, subtraction for change detection and DSA angiography, multiplication by masks), set and logical operations, clipping/scaling results back to 0-255
05 Geometric transformations and interpolation: spatial transformation plus intensity interpolation, affine transforms with homogeneous 3x3 matrices (scale, rotate, translate, shear; recap deep-cse-5-2 lesson 09), forward vs inverse mapping (holes), nearest-neighbour, bilinear worked on a 2x2 patch, bicubic, zooming and shrinking, image registration with control points (tie points), perspective/projective warp for document scanning
06 Intensity transformations: point processing s = T(r), image negative s = L-1-r, log transform s = c log(1+r) (Fourier spectrum display), power-law (gamma) s = c r^gamma worked with gamma 0.4 and 2.5, gamma correction for displays, piecewise-linear contrast stretching worked, thresholding as a limit case, intensity-level slicing, bit-plane slicing worked (pixel 194 to 8 bits), look-up tables
07 Histogram processing: histogram h(rk) = nk, normalised p(rk) = nk/MN, dark/bright/low/high contrast shapes, histogram equalisation derivation idea (CDF) and the classic 3-bit 64x64 worked example (790, 1023, 850, 656, 329, 245, 122, 81 -> 1, 3, 5, 6, 6, 7, 7, 7), why the discrete result is not perfectly flat, histogram matching (specification) worked, local histogram processing, CLAHE idea, histogram statistics (mean, variance) for local enhancement
08 Spatial filtering fundamentals: neighbourhood + operation, kernels (masks, windows), correlation vs convolution (kernel rotated 180 degrees) worked on a 1D impulse and a 3x3 patch, padding (zero, replicate, mirror), output size, separable kernels and the cost saving (n^2 vs 2n) worked, linear vs nonlinear filters, how filters relate to the frequency domain (preview lesson 12)
09 Smoothing (lowpass) spatial filters: box (mean) filter worked on a 3x3 patch, weighted average, Gaussian kernel (sigma, size about 6 sigma), blur vs noise trade-off, order-statistic (nonlinear) filters: median worked on salt-and-pepper, max and min, why median keeps edges, real uses (skin smoothing, shadow removal before thresholding)
10 Sharpening (highpass) spatial filters: first vs second derivative on a 1D profile worked, Laplacian kernels (4- and 8-neighbour), sharpening g = f - c Laplacian worked, unsharp masking and highboost (k > 1), gradient magnitude with Sobel for enhancement, combining methods (noise amplification warning), sharpening in phone cameras
11 The 2D Fourier transform: why frequency (slow vs fast changes), 1D DFT recap (deep-cse-5-7 lesson 15), 2D DFT and inverse, spectrum and phase (phase carries structure: swap experiment), centring by multiplying with (-1)^(x+y), periodicity and symmetry, the DC term equals MN x mean, log display, 2D sampling and aliasing, convolution theorem and wraparound (zero padding to P >= A + C - 1), separability and FFT cost worked (N^2 log N vs N^4)
12 Filtering in the frequency domain: the 7-step procedure, ideal lowpass (cutoff D0, ringing explained), Butterworth (order n) and Gaussian lowpass, highpass versions H_hp = 1 - H_lp, Laplacian and unsharp masking in frequency, homomorphic filtering (illumination vs reflectance), notch filters for periodic noise and moire (scanned newspaper), when spatial is cheaper than frequency
13 Noise models and the degradation model: g = h * f + eta, sources of noise (sensor heat, low light, transmission), PDFs: Gaussian, Rayleigh, Erlang (gamma), exponential, uniform, impulse (salt-and-pepper), periodic noise, estimating noise parameters from a flat patch (mean and variance worked), signal-to-noise ratio, noise-only case (restoration by spatial filtering)
14 Restoration with spatial filters: arithmetic, geometric, harmonic and contraharmonic mean (Q > 0 kills pepper, Q < 0 kills salt) worked, order-statistic: median, max, min, midpoint, alpha-trimmed mean worked, adaptive local noise reduction filter, adaptive median filter (stage A and B) worked, choosing a filter for each noise type
15 Estimating degradation, inverse and Wiener filtering: estimating H by observation, experiment (impulse/point spread function) and modelling (atmospheric turbulence, linear motion blur), inverse filtering F = G/H and why noise explodes where H is small, cutoff radius trick, Wiener (minimum mean square error) filter with the constant K approximation, constrained least squares brief, deblurring a shaky phone photo
16 Colour fundamentals and colour models: light and colour (cones S, M, L), primary and secondary colours of light vs pigment, tristimulus and chromaticity diagram idea, RGB cube (24-bit, 16.7 million colours), CMY and CMYK, HSI model and RGB-to-HSI conversion worked with numbers, YCbCr recap (deep-cse-5-9 lesson 04), Lab brief, why HSI/HSV helps segmentation, pseudocolour (intensity slicing, e.g. thermal and weather maps)
17 Colour image processing: full-colour processing per channel vs vector, colour transformations (intensity, complements), colour slicing (cube and sphere), tone and colour corrections, white balance (gray-world idea worked), histogram equalisation on intensity only, colour smoothing and sharpening, colour segmentation (HSI hue mask, RGB Euclidean distance worked), colour edges brief, skin and leaf detection examples
18 Morphology I, binary basics: images as sets, structuring element (SE) and origin, reflection and translation, erosion worked on a small binary grid, dilation worked, duality, opening (erosion then dilation) and closing worked, what each removes (spurs, gaps, small holes), idempotence, choosing SE shape and size
19 Morphology II, algorithms and grayscale: hit-or-miss transform, boundary extraction (A minus erosion), hole filling by conditional dilation, extraction of connected components, connected-component labelling (two-pass) worked, thinning and skeletons, convex hull brief, pruning, grayscale erosion/dilation (min/max), opening/closing, morphological gradient, top-hat for uneven lighting (rice grains), granulometry idea
20 Point, line and edge detection: detecting discontinuities, point detection with the Laplacian, line detection kernels, edge models (step, ramp, roof) and derivatives, gradient magnitude and direction, Roberts, Prewitt and Sobel worked on a patch, why smoothing first, Marr-Hildreth LoG and zero crossings, Canny's three goals and four steps (Gaussian, gradient, non-maximum suppression, hysteresis thresholds) traced, edge linking, Hough transform for lines (rho = x cos theta + y sin theta) worked with three points, Hough for circles brief
21 Thresholding: segmentation basics, global thresholding and the role of noise and illumination, basic iterative global algorithm worked, Otsu's method (maximise between-class variance) worked on a small histogram, multiple thresholds, smoothing and edges to improve the histogram, variable/adaptive thresholding (local mean, Niblack/Sauvola idea), document scanning with uneven light
22 Region-based segmentation and clustering: region growing from seeds worked, split and merge with quadtrees, k-means on colours worked (recap deep-cse-6-1 lesson 21), superpixels (SLIC) idea, watershed segmentation (topographic view, markers, over-segmentation), graph-cut and active contours idea, segmentation evaluation (IoU and Dice worked), deep segmentation preview (deep-cse-8-3)
23 Wavelets and multiresolution processing: why multiresolution (small and large objects), image pyramids (Gaussian, Laplacian) and storage cost (4/3), subband coding idea, Haar transform worked on [9, 7, 3, 5], 2D DWT one level (LL, LH, HL, HH), multi-level decomposition, wavelet denoising by thresholding small coefficients (hard vs soft), edge and texture uses
24 Image compression: why compress (raw sizes worked), coding, spatial/temporal and irrelevant (psychovisual) redundancy, entropy and compression ratio worked, fidelity criteria (RMSE, PSNR worked), lossless methods recap (Huffman, run-length for fax, LZW, PNG predictive filters), lossy methods: block transform coding and JPEG recap (deep-cse-5-9 lessons 11-12), JPEG 2000 with wavelets, quality vs size choices for WhatsApp/Facebook uploads
25 Representation, description and features: boundary following, Freeman chain codes worked (first difference for rotation invariance), polygon approximation, signatures, boundary descriptors (perimeter, diameter, Fourier descriptors idea), regional descriptors (area, compactness 4 pi A / P^2 worked, Euler number), moments and Hu invariants idea, texture (statistical, GLCM worked on a tiny image), PCA for features (recap deep-cse-2-5 lesson 25), keypoint features (Harris, SIFT, HOG idea), bridge to Pattern Recognition (deep-cse-6-12)
26 Capstone and wrap-up: a Bangladeshi licence plate reader (invented "PlatePath" at a Dhaka toll plaza): requirements, capture and gamma correction, grayscale and denoising, contrast enhancement, edge/morphology plate localisation, perspective correction, adaptive thresholding, connected components for characters, features and recognition (template matching vs a learned classifier), Bangla digit challenges, evaluation, failure cases (night, rain, motion blur), course recap and what next (deep-cse-6-12, deep-cse-8-3, deep-cse-8-1)
