# Course 5: Electrical Circuits  (level id "deep-cse-1-5", lesson ids "deep-cse-1-5-01".."-25")
01 What electricity is: charge, electrons, coulomb, conductors/insulators/semiconductors, water analogy, what a circuit is, symbols
02 Current, voltage, power and energy: definitions and units, DC vs AC preview, passive sign convention, P = VI, energy in kWh, electricity bill
03 Resistance and Ohm's law: resistivity R = ρL/A, conductance, temperature effect, resistor colour code, V = IR, power P = I²R = V²/R, linear vs non-linear
04 Circuit elements and sources: active vs passive, ideal and practical voltage/current sources, internal resistance, dependent sources (VCVS, CCVS, VCCS, CCCS), open and short circuit
05 Nodes, branches, loops and Kirchhoff's laws: KCL, KVL, sign rules, worked examples
06 Series and parallel resistors: equivalent resistance, combinations, step-by-step reduction, worked problems
07 Voltage divider and current divider rules: formulas, loading, real uses (sensors, volume knob)
08 Wye-Delta transformation and bridge circuits: Y-Δ / Δ-Y formulas, balanced Wheatstone bridge, measuring unknown R
09 Measuring instruments and the lab: ammeter, voltmeter, ohmmeter, multimeter, loading effect, breadboard, lab safety
10 Nodal analysis: reference node, node voltages, KCL equations, voltage sources and the supernode
11 Mesh analysis: mesh currents, KVL equations, current sources and the supermesh, nodal vs mesh choice
12 Linearity, superposition and source transformation: linear circuits, one source at a time, V-R series <-> I-R parallel
13 Thevenin's and Norton's theorems: finding V_th, R_th, I_N, with dependent sources, worked examples
14 Maximum power transfer: R_L = R_th, P_max = V_th²/4R_th, efficiency 50%, where it matters
15 Capacitors: capacitance, C = εA/d, q = Cv, i = C dv/dt, energy ½CV², series/parallel, DC behaviour
16 Inductors: magnetic field basics, v = L di/dt, energy ½LI², series/parallel, DC behaviour, no sudden current jump
17 First-order circuits (RC and RL): natural response, step response, time constant τ = RC, L/R, switching problems
18 Second-order circuits (RLC): series/parallel RLC, characteristic equation, overdamped/critically damped/underdamped
19 AC fundamentals: sinusoids, amplitude, frequency, period, phase, average and RMS values, 50 Hz mains in Bangladesh
20 Complex numbers, phasors and impedance: rectangular/polar form, phasor idea, Z of R, L, C, reactance, admittance
21 Sinusoidal steady-state analysis: Ohm/KVL/KCL with phasors, series-parallel impedances, nodal/mesh/Thevenin in AC
22 AC power: instantaneous, average (real), reactive, apparent power, power factor, power triangle, PF correction
23 Resonance and frequency response: series/parallel resonance, quality factor, bandwidth, simple RC/RL filters, radio tuning
24 Magnetically coupled circuits and transformers: mutual inductance, dot convention, ideal transformer, turns ratio, chargers
25 Three-phase systems, house wiring and safety: balanced 3-phase, Y and Δ, line vs phase voltage, house wiring, fuse, MCB, earthing, shock safety
