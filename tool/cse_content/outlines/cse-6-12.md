# Course: Pattern Recognition  (level id "deep-cse-6-12", lesson ids "deep-cse-6-12-01".."-26")
Builds on Linear Algebra (deep-cse-2-5: vectors, dot product, eigenvalues, lesson 25 PCA preview), Probability & Statistics
(deep-cse-2-6: Bayes' theorem, Gaussian distribution, mean and variance), Signals & Systems / DSP (deep-cse-3-7, deep-cse-5-7: correlation),
Algorithms (deep-cse-3-1: dynamic programming), Theory of Computation (deep-cse-3-5: grammars), Digital Image Processing (deep-cse-6-4:
segmentation, features) and above all Machine Learning (deep-cse-6-1: lessons 12 confusion matrix/ROC, 15 k-NN, 16 naive Bayes, 18 ensembles,
19 SVM, 21 k-means, 22 hierarchical clustering, 23 PCA). Those ML lessons are only recapped; here the focus is the classical statistical
pattern recognition view (Duda-Hart-Stork / Theodoridis style): Bayes decision theory and risk, discriminant functions, density estimation,
linear discriminants (perceptron, LMS, Fisher), feature extraction/selection, sequences (DTW, HMM), structural methods, mixtures/EM,
error estimation and classifier fusion. Later courses go deeper and are only pointed to: Data Mining (deep-cse-7-6), Neural Networks &
Fuzzy Systems (deep-cse-7-8), Deep Learning (deep-cse-8-1), NLP (deep-cse-8-2), Computer Vision (deep-cse-8-3). Every worked number is
checked with Python; Bangladesh examples (ilish vs sardine at a fish market, Bangla digits, bKash fraud, NID face/fingerprint).
01 What pattern recognition is: pattern, class, sample, feature, the "ilish vs sardine" sorting-line story, the PR system pipeline (sensing, preprocessing, segmentation, feature extraction, classification, post-processing), design cycle (data, features, model, training, evaluation), supervised vs unsupervised vs semi-supervised, statistical vs structural vs neural approaches, PR vs ML, course map
02 Features, feature space and distances: feature types (nominal, ordinal, interval, ratio), feature vector and feature space, decision regions and boundaries, what makes a good feature (discriminative, invariant, robust, cheap), normalisation (min-max and z-score worked), distance and similarity (Euclidean, Manhattan, Chebyshev, cosine worked), why scale matters worked
03 Probability refresher for PR: random variables, joint, marginal, conditional, independence, Bayes' rule with prior/likelihood/evidence/posterior worked, PDFs vs probabilities, 1D Gaussian worked, multivariate Gaussian, mean vector and covariance matrix worked on 4 points, correlation and what the covariance shape means
04 Bayesian decision theory I, minimum error: the decision problem, deciding from priors only, from likelihoods, posterior rule worked (ilish weight example), probability of error, Bayes error as the floor, likelihood ratio test worked, decision regions in 1D, many classes
05 Bayesian decision theory II, risk and loss: actions and loss matrix, conditional risk worked (dengue test example), Bayes risk, zero-one loss gives minimum error, threshold shifts with costs worked, reject option worked, Neyman-Pearson (fix false alarm rate), minimax idea
06 Discriminant functions and the Gaussian case: g_i(x) forms (posterior, log form), dichotomizer, Gaussian log discriminant, case 1 Sigma = sigma^2 I (minimum distance classifier), case 2 shared Sigma (Mahalanobis, linear boundary), case 3 arbitrary Sigma (quadratic boundary), 1D boundary worked with unequal variances, Mahalanobis distance worked
07 Maximum likelihood estimation: why estimate parameters, likelihood vs probability, log-likelihood, MLE for Bernoulli worked, MLE for Gaussian mean and variance worked, biased variance and n-1, MLE for multivariate Gaussian, plug-in classifier, overfitting with little data
08 Bayesian estimation and MAP: parameter as random variable, prior x likelihood, Beta-Bernoulli worked (rain days), MAP vs MLE vs full Bayesian, Gaussian mean with known variance worked (shrink toward prior), Laplace smoothing as MAP, when the prior matters
09 Naive Bayes and Bayesian networks: recap of deep-cse-6-1-16, conditional independence assumption and parameter counting worked, Gaussian naive Bayes worked on 2 features, why it works despite wrong assumption, Bayesian belief networks (DAG, CPTs, chain rule) worked on a tiny 3-node net, inference idea
10 Nonparametric density estimation: why nonparametric, histogram estimate and bin width, the basic p = k/(nV) formula, Parzen windows (box kernel worked, Gaussian kernel), bandwidth trade-off, k-NN density estimation worked, Parzen classifier, cost and curse of dimensionality
11 Nearest neighbour rules: 1-NN, Voronoi tessellation, k-NN recap (deep-cse-6-1-15), Cover-Hart bound (1-NN error at most twice Bayes error, asymptotically) worked, distance-weighted voting, editing (Wilson) and condensing (Hart) worked idea, k-d tree speed-up idea, metric learning brief
12 Linear discriminant functions and the perceptron: g(x) = w.x + w0, geometry (w normal to the boundary, distance = g/||w|| worked), augmented vectors and normalised samples, perceptron criterion and update worked step by step, convergence theorem, non-separable data, multiclass (one-vs-rest, one-vs-one, linear machine) and ambiguous regions
13 Minimum squared error and LMS: setting Ya = b, pseudoinverse solution worked on 4 points, Widrow-Hoff LMS update worked, MSE vs perceptron (always converges but may not separate), Ho-Kashyap idea, link to logistic regression and neural nets
14 Fisher's linear discriminant: projection to one line, why projecting naively fails, between-class and within-class scatter, Fisher criterion J(w), w = Sw^-1 (m1 - m2) worked on small 2D data, choosing the threshold, multiclass LDA (at most c-1 directions), LDA vs PCA
15 Support vector machines in PR: recap of deep-cse-6-1-19 (margin 2/||w||, support vectors), dual form and why only dot products appear, kernel trick worked (polynomial kernel = feature map dot product), RBF kernel and gamma, soft margin C, multiclass SVM, SVM vs Fisher vs perceptron
16 Feature extraction with PCA: recap deep-cse-6-1-23, why extraction, PCA as max variance and min reconstruction error, worked 2D example (covariance, eigenvalues, explained variance, projection, reconstruction error), eigenfaces pipeline step by step, whitening, PCA limits, kernel PCA and ICA named
17 Feature selection: curse of dimensionality and peaking (Hughes) phenomenon, filter vs wrapper vs embedded, class-separability criteria (Fisher score worked, correlation), sequential forward and backward selection worked with a scoring table, plus-l take-away-r, branch and bound idea, mutual information brief
18 Template matching and dynamic time warping: templates, sum of squared differences and normalised cross-correlation worked, invariance problems, sequences of different speed, DTW recurrence and table worked on two short sequences, warping window, uses (signature, spoken word, gestures)
19 Hidden Markov Models I: Markov chains and transition matrix worked, hidden states vs observations (weather and umbrella), lambda = (A, B, pi), the three problems (evaluation, decoding, learning), forward algorithm worked step by step, why brute force is N^T
20 Hidden Markov Models II: decoding with Viterbi worked with backpointers, learning with Baum-Welch idea (EM), left-to-right models, HMMs in speech and handwriting recognition, numerical underflow and logs, where neural sequence models took over
21 Clustering I, criteria and partitional methods: why cluster, similarity measures, sum-of-squared-error criterion, k-means recap (deep-cse-6-1-21), fuzzy c-means memberships worked, k-medoids, number of clusters, cluster validity (SSE, silhouette worked), hierarchical recap (deep-cse-6-1-22)
22 Gaussian mixture models and EM: mixture density, latent labels, responsibilities (E step) worked in 1D, update means/variances/weights (M step) worked, log-likelihood rises each step, k-means as hard EM, initialisation and local optima, choosing number of components (BIC idea)
23 Structural and syntactic pattern recognition: when shape structure matters, primitives and strings (chain codes worked), grammars recap (deep-cse-3-5), parsing a shape as a sentence, string edit distance worked, graph matching idea, attributed grammars, statistical vs structural trade-off
24 Error estimation and classifier evaluation: true error vs apparent error, holdout, k-fold and leave-one-out recap, bootstrap, confusion matrix recap, ROC and AUC recap, biometrics FAR, FRR and EER worked, DET curves idea, comparing two classifiers (McNemar test worked), reporting honestly
25 Combining classifiers: why combine, diversity, fusion at feature/score/decision level, majority vote accuracy worked, sum/product/max rules worked, weighted voting, bagging and boosting recap (deep-cse-6-1-18), cascade classifiers (Viola-Jones face detection idea), multimodal biometrics
26 Applications and capstone: Bangla handwritten digit recognition pipeline end to end (scanning, preprocessing, features, classifier, evaluation), face recognition (detection, alignment, features, matching), fingerprint minutiae matching, speaker/speech recognition, fraud detection, the deep learning shift (features learned not designed), ethics and bias, course recap and what next (deep-cse-7-6, deep-cse-7-8, deep-cse-8-1, deep-cse-8-2, deep-cse-8-3)
