# Course: Electronic Devices & Circuits  (level id "deep-cse-2-4", lesson ids "deep-cse-2-4-01".."-25")
01 Electronics warm-up: voltage, current, resistance, power, Ohm's law, KVL/KCL, R/C/L, AC vs DC, analog vs digital
02 Semiconductor materials: conductors, insulators, semiconductors, atoms and covalent bonds, energy bands, band gap
03 Intrinsic and extrinsic semiconductors: electrons and holes, doping, n-type and p-type, majority/minority carriers, mass-action law
04 How carriers move: drift, mobility, conductivity, diffusion, recombination, temperature effects
05 The PN junction: depletion region, barrier potential, forward and reverse bias, breakdown (avalanche, Zener)
06 Diode characteristics and models: I-V curve, Shockley equation, ideal / constant-drop / piecewise-linear models, load line, Q-point
07 Rectifiers: half-wave, full-wave centre-tap, bridge, peak inverse voltage, average and RMS values
08 Filters and the DC power supply: capacitor filter, ripple, block diagram of a phone charger / adapter
09 Wave-shaping with diodes: clippers (series, shunt, biased), clampers, voltage doublers
10 Zener diode and voltage regulation: Zener characteristic, simple shunt regulator design, line and load regulation
11 Special diodes: LED, photodiode, solar cell, Schottky, varactor, laser diode, optocoupler
12 The BJT: npn/pnp structure, how it works, IE = IC + IB, alpha and beta
13 BJT configurations and characteristics: CB, CE, CC; input/output curves; cut-off, active, saturation regions
14 BJT DC biasing: fixed bias, emitter-stabilised bias, voltage-divider bias, Q-point stability
15 BJT as a switch: driving LEDs, relays and motors, saturation design, logic inverter
16 BJT small-signal amplifiers: re model, CE amplifier gain, input/output impedance, coupling and bypass capacitors
17 Field-effect transistors and the JFET: structure, pinch-off, Shockley's equation, FET vs BJT
18 The MOSFET: enhancement and depletion, nMOS/pMOS, threshold voltage, cut-off / triode / saturation regions
19 MOSFET biasing and amplifiers: transconductance, common-source amplifier, MOSFET as a switch
20 CMOS circuits: CMOS inverter, NAND and NOR, power use, why CMOS rules chips, Moore's law
21 Frequency response and multistage amplifiers: decibels, cutoff frequency, bandwidth, cascading stages
22 Feedback and oscillators: negative feedback benefits, positive feedback, Barkhausen criterion, RC phase-shift, crystal, 555 timer
23 The operational amplifier: ideal op-amp, open-loop gain, differential amplifier, virtual short, CMRR, slew rate
24 Op-amp circuits: inverting, non-inverting, follower, summing, difference, integrator, differentiator, comparator, Schmitt trigger
25 From devices to chips: IC fabrication basics, logic families (TTL vs CMOS), ADC and DAC, course recap
