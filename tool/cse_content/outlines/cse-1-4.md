# Course 4: Calculus & Differential Equations  (level id "deep-cse-1-4", lesson ids "deep-cse-1-4-01".."-25")
01 Functions and graphs: domain, range, types (polynomial, rational, exponential, log, trig, inverse), composition, even/odd
02 Limits: intuitive idea, one-sided limits, limit laws, factoring/rationalizing tricks, limits at infinity, sin x / x
03 Continuity: definition (3 conditions), types of discontinuity, continuity of standard functions, Intermediate Value Theorem
04 The derivative: slope of tangent, limit definition, rate of change, notation, differentiability vs continuity (|x|)
05 Differentiation rules: constant, power, sum, product, quotient; derivatives of trig, exponential and log functions
06 Chain rule, implicit differentiation, logarithmic differentiation, derivatives of inverse trig, parametric derivatives
07 Higher-order and successive derivatives: nth derivatives of standard functions, Leibniz theorem
08 Mean value theorems: Rolle's theorem, Lagrange's MVT, Cauchy's MVT, geometric meaning and checks
09 Expansion of functions: Taylor and Maclaurin series of e^x, sin x, cos x, ln(1+x), (1+x)^n; approximation and error idea
10 Indeterminate forms and L'Hospital's rule: 0/0, inf/inf, 0*inf, inf-inf, 1^inf, 0^0, inf^0
11 Curve analysis: increasing/decreasing, local and absolute max/min, first and second derivative tests, concavity, inflection, asymptotes, curve sketching
12 Applied differentiation: optimization word problems, related rates, linear approximation and differentials, Newton's method
13 Functions of several variables: partial derivatives, higher partials, total differential, homogeneous functions and Euler's theorem
14 Multivariable tools: chain rule, gradient and directional derivative, Jacobian, max/min of two variables, Lagrange multipliers
15 Indefinite integrals: antiderivatives, basic formula table, constant of integration, substitution method
16 Integration by parts: LIATE rule, repeated and tabular parts, cyclic integrals, reduction formula idea
17 More integration techniques: trigonometric integrals, trigonometric substitution, partial fractions
18 The definite integral: Riemann sums, properties, Fundamental Theorem of Calculus (both parts), definite integral tricks
19 Improper integrals and special functions: infinite limits, infinite integrands, convergence, Gamma and Beta functions
20 Applications of integration: area between curves, volume by disk/washer/shell, arc length, average value, work
21 Multiple integrals: double integrals, iterated integrals, change of order, polar coordinates, triple integrals, area/volume
22 Introduction to differential equations: ODE vs PDE, order, degree, linear vs nonlinear, formation, general/particular solutions, IVP
23 First-order ODEs: separable, homogeneous, exact, integrating factors, linear first order, Bernoulli
24 Applications of first-order ODEs and Euler's method: growth/decay, Newton's cooling, mixing tanks, RC circuits, numerical solution
25 Second-order linear ODEs: constant coefficients, characteristic equation (3 cases), undetermined coefficients, variation of parameters, spring-mass and RLC
