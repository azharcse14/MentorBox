# Course: Professional Ethics & Cyber Law  (level id "deep-cse-6-6", lesson ids "deep-cse-6-6-01".."-25")
Builds on Software Engineering (deep-cse-4-4: requirements, testing, process), Database Management Systems (deep-cse-4-2: what data is stored),
Computer Networks (deep-cse-4-3), Human Computer Interaction (deep-cse-5-5: dark patterns, accessibility), Machine Learning (deep-cse-6-1:
training data, models) and Computer Security & Cryptography (deep-cse-6-2: attacks, hashing, signatures, PKI). Those are only recapped; here the
focus is what is right, what is legal, and how to decide when the two are unclear. Later courses go deeper, so keep these light and point forward:
Technical Writing (deep-cse-6-8), Software Project Management (deep-cse-6-11), Research Methodology (deep-cse-7-7), Blockchain (deep-cse-9-2),
Advanced Cyber Security (deep-cse-9-5). Quinn "Ethics for the Information Age" / Baase "A Gift of Fire" style, with case studies and small worked
numbers (fines, fairness rates, hashes) checked with Python. Bangladesh law (ICT Act 2006, Digital Security Act 2018, Cyber Security Act 2023 and
its later replacement) is stated only where sure and kept version-neutral: laws change, so always check the current text. Not legal advice.
01 What ethics is and why engineers need it: morals vs ethics vs law vs etiquette, ethics as reasoned argument, why computing raises new questions (scale, speed, copying, invisibility, policy vacuum idea of Moor), course map
02 Ethical theories I, consequences: subjective and cultural relativism and their problems, divine command brief, utilitarianism (Bentham, Mill), act vs rule utilitarianism, worked utility table, strengths and weaknesses
03 Ethical theories II, duties, character and contracts: Kantian deontology (categorical imperative, universal law, people as ends), rights, social contract (Hobbes, Rawls veil of ignorance), virtue ethics, comparing theories on one case
04 Making ethical decisions: a step-by-step framework, stakeholders, options, testing options (harm, publicity, reversibility, front-page test), worked case (hidden bug before launch), common traps (rationalisations)
05 Professionalism: what makes a profession (knowledge, autonomy, code, public trust), certification vs licensing, professional bodies (ACM, IEEE, BCS Bangladesh Computer Society, BASIS), competence, duty of care
06 Codes of ethics: ACM Code 2018 structure and key clauses, IEEE Code, ACM/IEEE-CS Software Engineering Code (8 principles), using a code on a case, limits of codes
07 Workplace dilemmas: conflicts of interest, gifts and bribery, confidentiality, loyalty, whistleblowing (when justified, steps, protections), harassment and fair treatment, remote and freelance work ethics
08 Reliability, liability and accountability: why software fails, Therac-25, Ariane 5, Boeing 737 MAX MCAS, negligence, warranties and disclaimers, product liability, the many-hands problem, what good engineers do
09 Academic integrity and plagiarism: types (copy, mosaic, self, contract cheating), code plagiarism and detection (MOSS idea), citing and referencing, collaboration rules, AI tools honestly, research misconduct (fabrication, falsification)
10 Intellectual property I, copyright: why IP exists, what copyright protects (expression not idea), automatic protection, rights given, term, fair use/fair dealing, software copyright, Berne and TRIPS, Bangladesh copyright law
11 Intellectual property II, patents, trademarks, trade secrets: patent tests (novel, non-obvious, useful), 20-year term, software patents debate, trademarks and domain names, trade secrets and NDAs, industrial designs, Bangladesh DPDT
12 Software licensing and open source: EULA and proprietary licences, free vs open source, permissive (MIT, BSD, Apache) vs copyleft (GPL, LGPL, AGPL), licence compatibility, Creative Commons, piracy, worked dependency check
13 Privacy foundations: what privacy is, why it matters, Warren and Brandeis, informational privacy, surveillance and data brokers, the 'nothing to hide' argument, re-identification and k-anonymity (Sweeney), metadata
14 Data protection law: fair information practices, GDPR principles, lawful bases, data subject rights, 72-hour breach notice, fines worked, other laws (India DPDP), Bangladesh status (version-neutral)
15 Privacy engineering: privacy by design, data minimisation, purpose limitation in code, pseudonymisation vs anonymisation, encryption and hashing recap, retention, consent UX and dark patterns, DPIA, differential privacy idea
16 Computer crime: what it is, categories (unauthorised access, malware, ransomware, DoS, fraud, phishing, identity theft, cyberbullying, child sexual abuse material), hacker types and motives, famous cases (Morris worm, WannaCry, Bangladesh Bank heist)
17 Cyber law foundations: why special laws, US CFAA, UK Computer Misuse Act 1990, Budapest Convention 2001, jurisdiction and cross-border crime, mutual legal assistance, intermediary liability
18 Bangladesh cyber law I, ICT Act 2006: legal recognition of electronic records and signatures, Controller of Certifying Authorities, offences, Cyber Tribunal, Section 57 controversy, related laws (Telecommunication Act 2001, Pornography Control Act 2012, RTI Act 2009)
19 Bangladesh cyber law II, from Digital Security Act to Cyber Security Act and after: DSA 2018 aims and criticism, CSA 2023 changes, later replacement, kinds of offences, free speech balance, how to stay safe and how to report (version-neutral)
20 Digital forensics and evidence: what digital evidence is, chain of custody, hashing to prove integrity (worked), order of volatility, write blockers and imaging, logs, admissibility, privacy of the investigated
21 Ethical hacking and security research: authorisation, scope and rules of engagement, penetration test phases, responsible vs full disclosure, bug bounties, CVE, legal risks, worked engagement letter
22 E-commerce, e-contracts and digital trust: electronic contracts (click-wrap, browse-wrap), digital signatures and PKI recap, consumer protection, online fraud, mobile financial services, spam
23 Free speech, content moderation and misinformation: speech and its limits, hate speech, defamation, platform moderation at scale, misinformation spread, deepfakes, online harassment, children online
24 AI ethics and algorithmic fairness: bias sources, COMPAS and hiring cases, fairness metrics worked (demographic parity, equal opportunity), transparency and explainability, accountability, generative AI issues, EU AI Act risk tiers
25 Social impact and capstone: digital divide, automation and jobs, environment and e-waste, accessibility, a full worked capstone case (a Dhaka health app) using every tool of the course, course recap and what next
