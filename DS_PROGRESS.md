# Data Science full courses: progress

Goal: every Data Science course as its own level, English and Bangla, same shape as the CSE courses (see CSE_PROGRESS.md).
The "Data Science" category (`ds`, assets/content/{en,bn}/ds.json) lists the course names only; no lessons written yet.

Status: `done` = full course in the app, `in progress` = lessons being written, `todo` = not started.
